## 1. Overview

Platform Admin is the internal portal for platform operators: people
management (Users, Astrologers, Assistants), service management, payments and
finance, disputes and complaints, remedies, content, reviews, availability,
activity history, and documents.

**Documentation ownership:** Platform Admin documentation is intended to be
the central authority for platform-wide rules. Under the current root
`CLAUDE.md`, the User App and Astrologer App review lists remain the official
decision logs for their own decisions, and Platform documents reference them
(see [governance](00-platform-governance.md), conflict PC-10).
