## 1. Purpose

Standalone module for viewing and moderating Astrologer content. The
Astrologer Profile → Content Management tab is view-only; full actions are here.

## 2. Content model (consistent with Astrologer `AD-138` – `AD-144`)

- Content types: **Text posts** and **Image posts**. **No video.**
- Audience: Public · Followers · Subscribers · subscription tiers (e.g. Silver, Gold).
- Publishing: **Publish Now · Schedule · Draft.**
- Astrologer content management includes: post creation, edit, publish,
  schedule, draft, likes, comments, content history.
- Subscriber benefits: **Daily Predictions** and **Weekly Predictions** are
  managed within Content Management (the Astrologer App also defines Monthly
  and Special Predictions — `AD-140`).

## 3. Overview

Total Posts · Published · Scheduled · Drafts · Hidden.

## 4. Admin actions

- View
- Flag
- Review
- Hide inappropriate content

Hidden content remains available to Admin. PDF / Excel export.

## 5. Not in scope

No content monetization and no video requirements are approved.
